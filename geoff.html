Geoff Laskowski

Geoff is a visual artist who lives and works in Saint Paul, Minnesota. He studied art at Saint John's University in Collegeville, Minnesota focusing primarily in photography. He is grateful to now have the space at Rock9 to explore painting as a method of artistic expression and idea exploration. He lives in Saint Paul and when he's not taking photos, painting, riding his bike, or playing his guitar, he works as a technology professional.
